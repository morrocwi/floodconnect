# Thai shelter/community sustainment — real-evidence mechanism replay

**Date of evidence:** 28 Sep 2026 Bangkok field reports + published Thai community research  
**Simulation:** NO  
**Counterfactual outcome claim:** NO  
**Purpose:** test whether FloodConnect's decision logic can represent observed Thai failure
mechanisms without fabricating flood depth, stock quantity, route speed or shelter capacity.

## Evidence mechanisms replayed

1. **Electricity can remain while service water fails.**
   An occupied residential site is therefore not treated as fully viable merely because power
   exists.

2. **A community/religious building can itself be flood-exposed.**
   Building identity never overrides current site-hazard screening.

3. **A dry kitchen can move food toward flooded households.**
   The kitchen is represented as a support/service node, not automatically as a shelter.

4. **Older adult living alone is a relationship problem, not an age score.**
   Age triggers assessment; declared medicine/mobility/communication needs create specific
   support requirements; living alone creates a buddy/reassessment-link check.

5. **Composition matters.**
   A child living only with an older adult is tagged for assessment, but is not automatically
   declared unsafe or non-viable.

## Interpretation boundary

Passing these tests means the architecture can encode the observed mechanism with fail-closed
logic. It does **not** demonstrate that FloodConnect improved the historical response, reduced
harm, optimized allocation, or would have chosen the correct action for every affected household.
